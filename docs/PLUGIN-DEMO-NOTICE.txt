MORPHEUS AUDIO PLUGIN — DEMO BUILD
==================================

What this is
------------
A prebuilt Morpheus audio plugin, published so you can hear what the plugin route
produces before you compile one of your own. It is the whole rig: a Marshall JCM 800
2203 captured in four states, through a Marshall G12M 4x12 captured with four mics,
into a drive, a three-band tone stack, a delay and a spring reverb — nine blocks, in
the order a pedalboard would have them.

ALL FOUR CAPTURES AND ALL FOUR CABINETS ARE IN THIS BUILD. They are the project
owner's own captures — his amp, his cabinet — included with the build; no third-party
licence covers them. To switch between them, use the Capture and Speaker controls in
the plugin's own panel — or the host's parameter list, where they are the last two
parameters, of the same names. Capture chooses the amp's state (Crunch, Crunch 2,
Hi Gain (TS), Hi Gain (RAT)); Speaker chooses the mic on the cabinet (545, U87,
017 Tube, M160). The demo opens on the first of each.

It is NOT the product. What Morpheus makes for you is a plugin built in your own
repository, around your own model, with your own cabinet — see morpheus.nz.

Where to put it
---------------
  macOS    VST3  ->  ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/
           AU    ->  ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/
           CLAP  ->  ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/CLAP/
           standalone: run it, no installation
  Windows  VST3  ->  C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3\
           CLAP  ->  %COMMONPROGRAMFILES%\CLAP\
  Linux    VST3  ->  ~/.vst3/          CLAP -> ~/.clap/
  (AArch64 only. 64-bit ARM Linux — a Raspberry Pi 4 or 5, for instance.)

These builds are unsigned. macOS will quarantine them, and Windows SmartScreen
will warn once. See the user manual for what each platform's warning means and
how to get past it.

Two noises that are not faults
------------------------------
THE STANDALONE MONITORS YOUR INPUT. It passes whatever your microphone or interface
hears through the plugin and out of your speakers, amplified by the amp model — so
with a live microphone and the speakers up it is a feedback loop, and it will howl.
Use headphones, or mute the input, or use the VST3/AU/CLAP inside a DAW instead:
there the host decides monitoring and this cannot happen.

AND THIS IS A CAPTURED HIGH-GAIN AMPLIFIER, WHICH IS HONEST ABOUT IT. With no input
at all it has a noise floor of its own, and the GATE ships at its lowest setting —
which is OFF, not merely gentle — so nothing is gating that floor between notes.
That is the capture you asked for, not a defect in the plugin. Raise GATE to about
-40 dB and it goes quiet when you stop playing, exactly as a real gate would.

The rig in this build
---------------------
The four captures are the project owner's own recordings of his JCM 800 2203, and the
four impulse responses are his own captures of the G12M 4x12. They are the owner's own
work and are included with this build; no third-party licence applies to them and none
is claimed for them. They are not third-party material.

The engine that plays a capture is NeuralAmpModelerCore, which is MIT licensed. The
